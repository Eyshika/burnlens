# Code of conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith, critique code and ideas rather than people,
and welcome newcomers.

Not acceptable: harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language or jokes, publishing
someone's private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who
break these rules. Report concerns privately through the contact on the maintainer's GitHub
profile (https://github.com/Eyshika).

This policy is adapted from the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
